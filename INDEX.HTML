Our Website helps in the awareness of the dangers,causes of deforestation and ways of how to prevent it. Deforestation causes soil erosion due to laeving the land bare.It also leads to climate change like increases temperatures. Deforestation also causes flooding it leads to death of animals and people.

AGRICUTURE

Forests are cleared to create land for growing crops and live stock grazing.

URBANIZATION

Forest land is cleared to build roads,houses,schools and industries.

FUELWOOD AND CHARCOAL PRODUCTION

People cut down trees to use as fire wood or make charcoal for cooking and heating
